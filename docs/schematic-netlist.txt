## Signal nets

SIM_TX:     SIM800L.TXD  --  ESP32.GPIO16
SIM_RX:     ESP32.GPIO17 --  SIM800L.RXD
SIM_RST:    ESP32.GPIO18 --  SIM800L.RST
SOIL1_AO:   SOIL1.AO     --  ESP32.GPIO1
SOIL2_AO:   SOIL2.AO     --  ESP32.GPIO2
DHT_DATA:   DHT11.DATA   --  ESP32.GPIO4
I2C_SDA:    AP3216.SDA   --  ESP32.GPIO5
I2C_SCL:    AP3216.SCL   --  ESP32.GPIO9
WATER_S:    WATER.S      --  ESP32.GPIO7
RLY1_IN_B:  ESP32.GPIO10 --  ULN2003.1_1B
RLY2_IN_B:  ESP32.GPIO11 --  ULN2003.2_2B
RLY1_IN:    ULN2003.16_1C -- RELAY1.IN
RLY2_IN:    ULN2003.15_2C -- RELAY2.IN
PUMP1+:     RELAY1.NO    --  PUMP1.+
PUMP2+:     RELAY2.NO    --  PUMP2.+

## Unused ULN pins (leave open)
ULN2003: 3,4,5,6,7,10,11,12,13,14

## Notes
- UART is crossed: module TXD -> MCU RX, module RXD -> MCU TX
- Relay IN never connects directly to GPIO
- C1 is parallel across SIM 5VIN/GND, close to module
- Pump 3V supply may stay isolated from control GND
